# Who
[Pratyabhijñā (RV-10.125)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Devi)

1. Śiva - Power of Consciousness (Cicchakti), I
2. Śakti - Power of Bliss (Ānandaśakti), Am, Pratyabhijñā
  > ANĀŚRITAŚIVA (unsupported)
3. Sadāśiva (indistinct universe), Abode of Will (Icchāśakti), Self
4. Īśvara (distinct universe), Abode of Omniscience (Jñānaśakti), Unself
5. Sadvidyā (continuity), Adobe of Omnipotence (Kriyāśakti), Action
  > ĀṆAVAMALA (primordial impurity of the conditioned)
  > VIJÑĀNĀKALA (conscious without actions)
6. Māyātattva (mā = to measure), veil on Śiva = differentiation by contraction, dissolution, deep sleep
  > KAÑCUKA (Sheats)
7. Kalā, agency: Spontaneous > costrained
8. Vidyā knowledge: Complete > incomplete
9. Rāga will: Fullness > desire
10. Kāla bliss: presence > duration
11. Niyati cit: Freedom > order
  > KĀRMAMALA stage (contractions)
12. Puruṣa, Śiva with Kañcuka, Conscious subject
13. Prakṛti, 3 Guṇa: Sattva, Rajas, Tamas (knowledge, will, action)
  > ANTAḤKARAṆA (Inner evolutes)
14. Buddhi - determination: sattva, knowledge
15. Ahaṅkāra - doer: rajas, will
16. Manas - thought: tamas, action
  > JÑĀNENDRIYA (perpceptions, sattvic)
17. Śrotra (hearing)
18. Tvak (touching)
19. Cakṣus (seeing)
20. Jihvā (tasting)
21. Ghrāṇa (smelling)
  > KARMENDRIYA (actions, rajasic)
22. Vāk (speaking)
23. Pāṇi (handling)
24. Pāda (locomotion)
25. Pāyu (excreting)
26. Upastha (genitals)
  > TANMĀTRA (subtle)
1.  Śabda (sound)
2.  Sparśa (touch)
3.  Rūpa (color)
4.  Rasa (flavor)
5.  Gandha (odor)
  > MAHĀBHŪTA (gross)

33. Vāyu (wind)
