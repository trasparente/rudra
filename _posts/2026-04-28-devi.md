# Devi
[Devi Suktam (RV-10.125)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Devi)

> I move with the forces of nature—the Rudras and the Vasus;
> 
> I accompany the Adityas and all the gods.
> 
> I uphold Mitra and Varuna,
> I sustain Indra and Agni, and the twin Ashvins.

> I bear the sacred Soma, pressed in the ritual;
> 
> I support Tvaṣṭṛ, Pūṣan, and Bhaga.
> 
> I bestow wealth upon the one who offers with devotion,
> 
> Upon the sacrificer who prepares and worships with sincerity.

> I am the Sovereign Queen, the gatherer of treasures,
> 
> The knower, the first among those worthy of worship.
> 
> The gods have established me in many places—
> 
> I pervade all beings and dwell in countless forms.

> Through me alone all eat their food,
> See, breathe, and hear what is spoken.
> 
> Even those who do not recognize me
> Live by me alone
> 
> —hear this, O you who listen with faith.

> I myself declare this truth,
> Honored by gods and humans alike:
> 
> Whomever I love, I make powerful—
> 
> I make them a sage, a seer, a knower of truth.

> I stretch the bow for Rudra,
> 
> That his arrow may strike the hater of truth.
> 
> I bring strength and struggle to the people,
> 
> And I pervade heaven and earth.

> I give birth even to the Father of this world,
> 
> My womb is in the cosmic waters, deep within the ocean.
> 
> From there I extend through all existence,
> 
> And I touch even the highest heavens.

> I blow like the wind, setting all worlds into motion;
> 
> Beyond heaven, beyond earth,
> 
> So vast am I in my greatness—
> 
> I exist, pervading all that is.
